# Course History

eLearn Studio keeps a record of changes to your course. If you delete a slide, overwrite content by mistake, or want to see what your course looked like at an earlier point, you can browse the history and restore a previous version.

---

## Viewing course history

1. Open your course in the editor.
2. Click the **History** button in the top toolbar (clock icon).
   A panel opens on the right showing a list of saved versions, newest first.
3. Click any version in the list to preview it.
   The canvas shows the course as it looked at that point in time.

---

## Restoring a previous version

1. Open the course history panel (see above).
2. Find the version you want to restore.
3. Click **Restore this version**.
   A confirmation dialog appears.
4. Click **Restore**.
   The course reverts to the selected version. The editor saves your current version as a new history entry before the restore, so you can undo the restore if needed.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Restoring a previous version replaces your current course content. The restore cannot be undone except by restoring again from history. Review the version carefully before confirming.

---

## When eLearn Studio saves your history

eLearn Studio saves a history entry:

- Every 10 minutes while you are actively editing
- When you click **Publish**
- When you add or delete a slide

> ℹ️ **Note:** History is stored for 90 days. Versions older than 90 days are removed automatically.

---

## Recovering a deleted slide

If you delete a slide and want it back:

1. Open the course history panel.
2. Find a version from before the slide was deleted.
3. Click **Restore this version**.
4. The deleted slide returns along with all the content it had at that time.

> 💡 **Tip:** If only one slide was deleted and you want to keep your other recent changes, restore the old version, copy the slide content, then restore to your current version and recreate the slide.
