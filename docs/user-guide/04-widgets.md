# Content Blocks

Content blocks are the building blocks of your slides. Drag them from the **Content Blocks** panel onto the canvas, then customize them using the **Properties** panel on the right.

---

## Adding a content block

1. Click the **Content Blocks** tab at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Find the block you want to add.
3. Drag it from the panel onto the canvas.
   The block appears on the slide and is automatically selected.
4. Move and resize it as needed.

---

## Text

The Text block adds a text box to your slide.

1. Drag a **Text** block onto the canvas.
2. Double-click the text box to start editing.
3. Type your content. Use the formatting bar that appears above the text box to apply bold, italic, font size, and color.
4. Click outside the text box to finish editing.

> 💡 **Tip:** Keep text on slides brief — aim for 30–50 words per slide. Detailed information belongs in a downloadable job aid, not on the slide itself.

---

## Image

The Image block places an image on your slide.

1. Drag an **Image** block onto the canvas.
2. In the Properties panel, click **Upload Image**.
3. Select an image from your computer (JPEG, PNG, or GIF).
   The image uploads and appears in the block.
4. Resize the block by dragging its handles.

> ℹ️ **Note:** Images are stored securely on the eLearn Studio server. You do not need to manage files manually.

---

## Button

The Button block adds a clickable button to your slide. Buttons can navigate to other slides or trigger course logic.

1. Drag a **Button** block onto the canvas.
2. In the Properties panel, enter the button label text.
3. Set the button action in the **Course Logic** section — see [Course Logic](06-actions-editor.md) for details.

---

## Shape

The Shape block adds a rectangle, circle, or other shape to your slide. Use shapes to create visual separators, highlight areas, or build custom layouts.

1. Drag a **Shape** block onto the canvas.
2. In the Properties panel, adjust the fill color, border color, and opacity.

---

## Media player

The Media Player block plays audio or video files on your slide.

1. Drag a **Media Player** block onto the canvas.
2. In the Properties panel, click **Upload Media**.
3. Select an audio (MP3) or video (MP4) file.
   The file uploads and the player appears on the slide.
4. Choose whether to auto-play when the slide loads, or require the learner to press Play.

> 💡 **Tip:** Always provide an alternative (such as a transcript) for audio and video content to support learners who cannot use audio.

---

## Navigation buttons

Navigation buttons let learners move between slides.

1. Drag a **Navigation Buttons** block onto the canvas.
   A pre-built set of **Previous**, **Next**, and **Home** buttons appears.
2. Move and resize the group as needed.
3. In the Properties panel, choose which buttons to show: Previous, Next, First, Last, or any combination.

> ℹ️ **Note:** The **Next** button on the last slide automatically triggers the course completion and sends the score to your LMS if you have a Done button on that slide.

---

## Score display

The Score Display block shows the learner's current score on the slide. Use it on a results or summary slide.

1. Drag a **Score Display** block onto the canvas.
2. It automatically shows the total score from all questions in the course.

---

## Done button

The Done button signals to your LMS that the learner has completed the course. Always place a Done button on your final slide.

1. Drag a **Done Button** block onto the canvas.
2. Resize and reposition it as needed.
3. When a learner clicks Done, the course sends the final score to the LMS and marks the course complete.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Without a Done button, your LMS may not record the learner's completion or score. Always include it on the last slide.

---

## Removing a content block

1. Click the content block on the canvas to select it.
2. Press the **Delete** key, or right-click and select **Delete**.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you delete a block by mistake, press **Ctrl+Z** (Windows) or **Cmd+Z** (Mac) to undo immediately.
