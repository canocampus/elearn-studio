# Working with Slides

Slides are the pages of your course. Each slide can contain any combination of content — text, images, questions, and simulations. This section covers how to add, organize, and manage slides.

---

## Adding a slide

1. Click the **New Slide** button in the top toolbar.
   The editor adds a blank slide after the currently selected slide.
2. The new slide is automatically selected and appears in the canvas.

> 💡 **Tip:** You can also click the **+** button at the top of the slide list to add a slide.

---

## Renaming a slide

Slide titles appear in the slide list and are used as section names in some LMS platforms.

1. Double-click the slide title in the slide list.
   The title becomes editable.
2. Type the new title.
3. Press **Enter** to confirm.

---

## Reordering slides

Drag a slide in the slide list to a new position. A blue line shows where the slide appears when you release it.

> 💡 **Tip:** Plan your slide order before you start adding content. Reordering slides later is easy, but reviewing the overall flow first saves time.

---

## Duplicating a slide

1. Right-click the slide in the slide list.
2. Select **Duplicate**.
   A copy of the slide appears immediately below the original.

Duplicating is useful when multiple slides share the same layout — create one, style it, then duplicate and change only the content.

---

## Deleting a slide

1. Click the slide you want to delete in the slide list to select it.
2. Click the **Delete Slide** button in the top toolbar.
   The slide is removed immediately.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Deleting a slide is permanent. Use [Course History](10-course-history.md) to restore a deleted slide if needed.

---

## Slide thumbnails

The slide list shows a small thumbnail of each slide. Thumbnails update as you make changes to the slide content.

If a thumbnail looks blank or out of date, click the slide to open it. The thumbnail updates after the slide is rendered.

---

## Slide templates

A slide template is a pre-designed background layout that you can apply to multiple slides. Templates are useful when all your slides share a common look — a header bar, footer, logo, or background color.

> ℹ️ **Note:** Slide templates are set up by your administrator or a designated course designer. Contact them to add or modify templates for your organization.

To apply a template to a slide:

1. Select the slide in the slide list.
2. In the right sidebar **Properties** panel, find the **Template** dropdown.
3. Select the template you want.
   The template's background elements appear on the slide. Your content sits on top of them.

Content added from templates is shared across all slides that use that template. Changing the template updates all slides using it at once.

---

## Navigating between slides

Click any slide in the slide list to open it in the canvas. The canvas always shows one slide at a time.

To see the full course flow, use the **Preview** button in the top toolbar.
