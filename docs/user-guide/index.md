# eLearn Studio — User Guide

Welcome to eLearn Studio, the authoring tool for creating interactive e-learning courses. Build your course one slide at a time, add rich content and questions, preview the result in a browser, and publish a SCORM package you can upload to your Learning Management System. This guide walks you through every feature, with screenshots, step-by-step instructions, and ready-to-use examples.

> **Targeting v0.5.62** — Actions Editor names fixed, UI stable.

---

## Quick start

- **Create a course.** Sign in and choose **New Course** from the top menu. An empty first slide appears.
- **Add a slide.** Click **Add slide** in the top toolbar; it becomes the active slide. Rename it by double-clicking its title in the slide list.
- **Add content.** Drag blocks from the **Blocks** tab on the left — text, images, buttons, questions — onto the canvas, then edit their settings in the **Props** tab on the right.
- **Publish.** Click **Publish SCORM** when you are ready. Choose a format, download the ZIP file, and upload it to your Learning Management System.
